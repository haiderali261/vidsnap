What is VidSnap?

VidSnap is a free online video downloader that supports 50+ platforms including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, Reddit, and many more. We help you save videos for personal offline use quickly and easily — no signup required.

Whether you want to save a funny TikTok, an Instagram Reel, a YouTube video, or a Twitter clip, VidSnap makes it as simple as pasting a link and clicking download.
