Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025
Short version: VidSnap does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. No account needed, no tracking beyond standard ad cookies.

Data We Collect

VidSnap does not collect any personal data. We do not require registration or login. The only data processed is the video URL you provide, which is used solely to fetch download links — it is not stored on our servers.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies only to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode). No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used beyond Google AdSense (when active). You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

VidSnap uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate VidSnap:

  • RapidAPI — processes download link requests
  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements
  • Vercel — hosts this website

Each of these services operates under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them independently.

Children's Privacy

VidSnap does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.
